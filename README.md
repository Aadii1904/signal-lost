# Signal Lost

A tiny endless runner for when the internet is down, in the spirit of Chrome's offline dinosaur.

A backpacker runs along the road. Jump over suitcases, road cones and low-flying paper planes; the road speeds up as you go.

- **Play:** press <kbd>Space</kbd>, <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>W</kbd>, or tap the game
- Your best score is kept in the browser
- Follows your system's light or dark mode

It's the offline page of [travellingcoder.in](https://travellingcoder.in): lose your connection on the site and this is what you get. You can also play it, and edit the code live, at [travellingcoder.in/projects/signal-lost](https://travellingcoder.in/projects/signal-lost).

## Files

- `index.html`: the page
- `style.css`: colours and layout
- `game.js`: the game (plain canvas, no libraries)

Open `index.html` in a browser to play; no build step.
